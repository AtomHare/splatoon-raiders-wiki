# Strategies

Boss and raid strategies, movement and combat tech, co-op team setups and
farming routes. Each strategy is one page in this folder.

Descriptions of bosses and enemies are on [Inkipedia](https://splatoonwiki.org);
a strategy page explains **how to beat them**.

## How to add a strategy

Copy `templates/strategy.md` into `docs/strategies/`, rename it (for example
`boss-name-solo.md`) and fill it in. It appears in the menu automatically.

Or [submit a strategy with the form](https://github.com/AtomHare/splatoon-raiders-wiki/issues/new?template=1-strategy.yml).
