## Bottomless Buffet Opening Spawn Layouts

**By:** [キタムラ](https://x.com/8oqXKYhkYOYV9Qy/status/2090441318115995688) · **Site:** [mugen-jikido-haichi.pages.dev](https://mugen-jikido-haichi.pages.dev/) · **Languages:** Japanese, English

Screenshots of the opening spawn layout of every Bottomless Buffet floor from 1
to 99, taken from the spawn position. You can browse them by stage or filter
floors by layout and by the Boss and Seasoned Salmonids present, which helps
you recognise a floor from its opening layout. It also lists a few layouts that
have not appeared by floor 99.

Corrections and suggestions go to the author as replies to
[their post on X](https://x.com/8oqXKYhkYOYV9Qy/status/2090441318115995688).
